Design (2026-11-07, maintainer's decision): in `simpleGeoCreatureBehavior.ts` the leader is authoritative. It walks its geodesic at spec speed every frame, and nothing the creature meets slows or turns it; the maintainer plans to add predetermined leader rules later (for example stopping when blocked), but for geodesic mice and butterflies there are none. The leader leaves a trail (`TrailPiece`, one straight piece per cell, with the portal map from the previous piece). The creature steers at a chase point on the trail, at most `creatureLeashMeters` plus this frame's reach ahead of it along the trail (along-track only) and never past the leader, and moves at up to `creatureCatchUpFactor` (1.5) times its speed, so a blocked creature goes round and catches up along the trail. `creatureFromChase` maps the chase point's cell frame to the creature's (composed with the trail's crossings and the creature's `cellFromStart`). Evidence: blocked for 10 s out of every 40 s, every example creature ends on its unblocked twin's pose to 1e-13 m (test 'catches up with where it would be unblocked').
Old bug: the leash held the leader itself, by straight-line distance. A geodesic passing a vertex closer than body + zone radius sends the creature wide; the leader froze behind the zone, the creature got ahead around the vertex, and `applyForbiddenZoneSideRule` turned every step back into a full-length tangent, so it circled the cone. The extra loop composed the vertex's holonomy (angle deficit: cube 90, dodecahedron 36, icosahedron 60, octahedron 120, tetrahedron 180 deg; measured exactly) into the leader-to-creature map, so the creature followed a rotated copy of its leader: another geodesic (cube: 10.8 m off for 46 s). Nothing self-corrects a wrong map; the design keeps it right by keeping the chase point close along the trail, so the creature never cuts across to a far target.
Still needed with the chase point: `isTargetBehindAroundZone` (target less than a quarter turn behind on the required way round a zone) stops the creature at the zone and lets the chase point move on, because the body centre (0.35 m ahead of the origin) can get ahead of it around the zone. Failed variants: a tangent . desired < 0 test deadlocks (head-on its sign is noise).
Probes: comparing the creature with `leaderForDebug` through its own map cannot see a wrong map. Check that the map's rotation is identity when creature and leader share a cell (not in single-cell worlds such as genus-2, where it blips for a frame at crossings), or the distance from the creature to the geodesic walked with `walkDevelopedSegment` (edge-running geodesics give false spikes; on a torus long walks fill the cell). Tests may not call `leaderForDebug` (hygiene rule H4). Guards: `tests/world-objects/simpleGeoCreatureStall.test.ts` and 'catches up with where it would be unblocked' in `simpleGeoCreature.test.ts`.
