Symptom: a mouse (or butterfly) passing very close to a corner glitches, then turns almost 360 degrees (dodecahedron top-geo-mouse at t~41.5 s; torus-geo-mouse at t~6.7 s, up to 416 deg in 5 s).
Root cause (in `simpleGeoCreatureBehavior.ts`): the geodesic passes the vertex at a few cm, inside body radius + zone radius, so the side rule sends the body round the zone on a ~0.8 m radius. The leash was the straight-line creature-leader distance (0.5 m), so it froze the leader behind the zone while the creature was merely off to the side. The creature then got ahead of the frozen leader around the vertex; every later step back toward it pointed into the zone, and `applyForbiddenZoneSideRule` replaced it by a full-length tangent in the required direction, so the creature orbited the whole cone (cone angle 324 deg on the dodecahedron) to reach the leader from behind.
Failed approaches: (1) stopping when tangent . desired < 0 deadlocks: the leader stays frozen behind; and in a head-on pass the dot is ~0 and noisy, so the creature waits for ever while the leader walks away. (2) An along-track leash alone still leaves mice making big turns (the 0.35 m forward collision offset lets the body get ahead around the zone).
Fix: the leash measures the lead along the leader's forward axis only; and `isTargetBehindAroundZone` (target less than a quarter turn behind on the required way round the zone centre, measured from the creature origin) makes the creature stop at the zone and sets `waitingForLeader`, which lets the leader advance whatever the leash says. A geodesic passing the vertex on the required side always moves round it in that direction, so the leader cannot run away. Head-on (target ~180 deg across) still goes round.
Evidence: 1800 s probe over every example creature: no turn > 216 deg in 5 s (before: up to 157 per creature), creature-leader distance <= 0.85 m (before 2.4), longest pause 0.37 s, no overlapping frames. Guard: `tests/world-objects/simpleGeoCreatureStall.test.ts` 'does not circle a vertex its geodesic passes closely'. Probe scripts: step behaviors at 1/60 s with `npx vite-node`, compare creature heading with the leader pose `composeRigidTransform3(creatureFromLeader, leader.localPose)` from `leaderForDebug` (tests may not use `leaderForDebug`: hygiene rule H4).
