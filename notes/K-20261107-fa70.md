Observed live 2026-11-07 in world tetrahedron: emitter:2 lay on face-c side-0 (signed distance -6.8e-15). traceGeodesicSegment / traceGeodesicPathForConnectionMode count a side reached within intersectionTolerance (1e-7) of the requested length as crossed (deliberate), so a chord ending on the side realized one extra portal letter; with connectEmitters:false (all chord rebuilds) this gave word-mismatch, and tieAtNewVertex rolled back with 'The tied geodesics could not be rebuilt.' rebuildConnectedGeodesicBetweenEmitters failed too. Arcs built by shooting were fine because connected mode stops on emitter-hit first.

Fix (src/world-objects/geodesic/intervals.ts, stopOnEndSide): on a mismatch, if the last trace is a portal-hit and the traces cover the chord length within tolerance, turn the last terminal into open; accept only if the word then matches. A blanket drop would be wrong: on a one-cell torus or with the anchor registered in the cell beyond the side, the end crossing is part of the correct word. Regression: tests/world-objects/geodesic/geodesicRebuildOutcomes.test.ts, 'an anchor exactly on a portal side'.

Not covered: a START anchor on a side with the chord leaving through that side (untested). How the emitter got onto the edge is unknown (guess: placed on a geodesic at a portal crossing).
