Observed live 2026-11-07 in world tetrahedron: emitter:2 at face-c (3.3960277334480233, -7.451240755088368) is on face-c side-0 (signed distance -6.8e-15). Arc emitter:1 -> emitter:2 with word d:1->a:0, a:1->b:1, b:0->c:2 was built in connected mode (terminal emitter-hit, so fine). Tie & detach at emitter:2 rewires the end to a vertex at the same point and calls rebuildDerivedGeodesicSegments -> realizeIntervalChord -> traceAndClassifyChord with connectEmitters:false. traceGeodesicSegment accepts side hits with t <= maxLengthMeters + intersectionTolerance (1e-7), so the last segment in face-c ends with portal-hit side-0 -> face-d, the realized word gains a 4th letter, kind word-mismatch, refusal 'The tied geodesics could not be rebuilt.' Both pick orders fail. Rollback works (state unchanged). Reproduced offline in tests/_temp/tieRepro.report.test.ts (run: npx vitest run --project report <file>). Unknown: how the emitter got exactly onto the edge (placement on a geodesic at a portal crossing is a guess). Any rebuild of an arc ending at an anchor on an edge is likely affected, not just tie.
