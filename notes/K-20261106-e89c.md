Review of job 66, by reading, not run. Job 66 moved pruneMissingGeodesicIntersectionObjects before updateGeodesicIntersectionObjects in crossingsTick. That does not change crossing ids. But crossingsTick subscribes to segments, so after the aiming stops no pass runs and the vanished marker stays remembered for as long as the scene is still. worldGeometryComputedObjects.ts line 81 prunes the memory and passes the forgotten ids to removeProtractorAnglesForMissingVertices, so a geometry commit during the wait deletes the hidden angle. The job Report says 'vanished in the very last pass', which is true but reads as a one-frame window.
