Observed: the render loop takes `deltaSeconds = clock.getDelta()` at src/render/three/createThreeApp.ts:1211 (step 28). Movement is scaled by that delta with no clamp visible in the grep output. The desktop input path `consumeFrame(deltaSeconds)` at src/render/three/desktopControls.ts:265 also passes it straight through (steps 56, 62).

Concluded: the step length is `speed * dt` and `dt` is unbounded, so a long frame produces a long single step. The single-step portal exit guess then skips crossings and can leave the body outside the target cell. This is the framerate-dependence item recorded in issues 62 and 64. The digest does not show a runtime reproduction of this effect.
