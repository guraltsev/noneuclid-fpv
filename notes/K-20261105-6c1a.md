**Observed:** A bench script compiled each example world (src/examples/*.world.js) and printed F, E, V, chi = V - E + F, 2*pi*chi, the summed curvature sumCurv, the number of junction cylinders, edge-length error, vertex error, reciprocal-portal error, and the per-vertex cone angles in degrees. For 001-basic-cube, 002-basic-tetrahedron, torus-moduli and fixed-dodecahedron, sumCurv equalled 2*pi*chi to 4 decimal places and the length, vertex and reciprocal errors were near zero (about 1e-15 or smaller). The torus-moduli world reports chi=0 with four 360-degree cone angles.

**Concluded:** Because the summed interior and boundary angle defects must equal 2*pi*chi for a valid gluing, a mismatch between sumCurv and 2*pi*chi in a world script is a cheap signal of a gluing or vertex-angle error. This is a self-test, not a proof, and it only checks what the script computes.
