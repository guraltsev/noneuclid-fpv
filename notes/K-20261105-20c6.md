Observed: a standalone Node micro-benchmark (run with --expose-gc) measured bytes per call. Clearing with `arr.length = 0` and then pushing allocated about 154-196 B/call in two runs and 480 B/call in a third, while writing by index allocated about 1.75-162 B/call.

Concluded: for a reused buffer on a hot path, writing by index with a tracked count is the lower-allocation choice; `length = 0` plus push is not a free reset. The result came from one machine and a few runs, so re-measure before relying on the exact numbers.
