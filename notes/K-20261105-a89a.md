Observed: after job 52 removed explicit 'refresh angle' calls on carry and replaced them with a wake rule, carrying a cannon with two locked beams left the protractor angle wedge at its old position (steps 88-92). A test that carries the cannon reproduces the failure at carry step 1 (step 108). Adding a cannon-kind wake to the angles tick fixed that case (steps 110-111). Turning the cannon was not affected (step 92).

Concluded: the angle tick's wake condition is a hand-maintained list of kinds, so any change to a dependency not on that list (such as the far end of a beam moving) can leave the angle stale. The hotfix only covered the reproduced case, and the user rejected it as a bandage (steps 120-126). A dependency-tracking approach replaced it (steps 122-130). The hand-written list is the root cause, not the cannon case alone.
