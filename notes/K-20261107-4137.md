Observed: after merging jobs 61 to 66 into design/012-engine-api, npm test in the main checkout failed. The hygiene H1 check reported test-like files under .worktrees/batch-61-66/tests/ and .temp/batch6166/ as living outside tests/. The scanner walks the filesystem with IGNORED_DIR_NAMES = [.git, node_modules, dist, .vite, coverage, assets-src], while .gitignore already lists /.worktrees/ and /.temp/. Adding ".temp" and ".worktrees" to IGNORED_DIR_NAMES made the suite pass again, including with a stray probe test placed in .temp/.

Concluded: the scanner is meant to mirror .gitignore, and it does not for these two folders. Any new worktree or scratch folder will break the check until it is ignored.
