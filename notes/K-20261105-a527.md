From a read-only audit on 2026-11-05 (the first point also read in the code).

- `worldClient.afterStep` and `geodesicCannonTools.afterWorldStep` walk all events since `endFrame`. When P8 runs several ticks per frame they must run once per frame, or take a cursor.
- The import rule in `scripts/docs/lib.mjs` allows modules only `portalWord`, `development`, `trace` and `objects` from `world-objects/geodesic/`; only `carry` and `geodesicModel` are excepted. `geodesicEmitters` needs model queries or its own exception.
- `createToolHelpLens` is unreachable as a whole: its only caller is after `if (!focus) return undefined` in `aimFeedback.ts`. Descriptor help text is therefore never shown.
- `coreTools` marks carry, rotate and aim as `main-tool` through `base`, which is wrong.
- `onEvents` delivery is lossy: events emitted in or after a module's derive slot are cleared by `endFrame` before that module's handler sees them at one step per frame.
- Read tracking for measurements is on by default (`trackReads`, design 014 D14). The flow+dependents bench scene has one length and one angle, the worst case for tracking; judge it on the `many lengths` lines. A full refresh won there (27.96 against 34.05 us/step) and lost at 32 untouched lengths.
- `getRememberedGeodesicIntersectionObject` is reached only by tests in practice, but a test pins the behaviour it gives (an angle hidden while its crossing is gone); job 52 changed that to deletion, an open question for the maintainer.
