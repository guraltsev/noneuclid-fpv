Added 2026-11-07. Before, dropping a carried emitter on a geodesic did nothing until a retrace (e.g. Extend) hit it. Now carry's endCarry (carry/detach and the leash/missing drop in the tick) dispatches the module-only geodesicEmitters/land, which dispatches geodesicModel/cutAtEmitter. cutArcsPassingEmitter: arcs with a segment in the emitter's cell within emitterConnectionToleranceMeters (0.3 m) of the beam point, both halves >= GEODESIC_MIN_LENGTH_METERS; skips arcs on the emitter itself and arcs whose segments do not run from the start anchor (checked by first segment start point and word), so free arcs with the emitter at the end role are NOT cut. Tied arcs ARE cut: the emitter becomes an end node of both parts and curve shortening straightens them independently (its only memo, `quiet`, is woken by arc writes; no per-curve state). Verified on the torus tied pair: after rest the vertex merges away and the pin keeps two connected arcs (tests/world-objects/geodesic/cutAtEmitter.test.ts). A merged arc may come out reversed (word read from the other end). The word is split by the segments' portal-hits before/after the hit; both halves are realized before writing, so a failure changes nothing. Free half intended length = its chord + what the old arc stopped short. Emitter yaw unchanged. Latent: placing a NEW emitter on a crossing (cutGeodesicsAtVertex) with a connected arc through it likely duplicates the continuation - not verified, not fixed.
