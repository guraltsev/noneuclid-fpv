Added 2026-11-07. Before, dropping a carried emitter on a geodesic did nothing until a retrace (e.g. Extend) hit it. Now carry's endCarry (both carry/detach and the leash/missing drop in the tick) dispatches the module-only geodesicEmitters/land, which dispatches geodesicModel/cutAtEmitter. cutArcsPassingEmitter: arcs with a segment in the emitter's cell within emitterConnectionToleranceMeters (0.3 m) of the beam point, both halves >= GEODESIC_MIN_LENGTH_METERS; skips arcs on the emitter itself, tied arcs, and arcs whose segments do not run from the start anchor (checked by first segment start point and word) - so free arcs with the emitter at the end role are NOT cut. The word is split by the segments' portal-hits before/after the hit; both halves are realized (realizeIntervalChord) before writing, so a failure changes nothing. Free half intended length = its chord + what the old arc stopped short. Emitter yaw is not changed. Latent: placing a NEW emitter on a crossing (cutGeodesicsAtVertex) with a connected arc through it likely duplicates the continuation - not verified, not fixed.
