Observed: the property test for static cull soundness (`tests/cell-complex/staticPortalPathCull.property.test.ts`) failed on `basicCube` root front with a word of seven portals reported as missing, even though the cull had kept it. After the failures at steps 109-117, the agent changed the loop in `tests/support/portalWordWitness.ts` to stop at `Math.min(result.crossings.length, maxCrossings)`, with a comment saying the walk records one crossing more than `maxCrossings` before it reports "crossing-limit". The three property tests then passed at step 121.

Concluded (agent's reading, not separately reproduced): the segment-walk result can contain one crossing beyond the configured limit, so a witness loop that counts up to `result.crossings.length` checks counts that exceed the limit and produces false "missing" reports.
