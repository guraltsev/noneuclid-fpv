Observed: the fuse test in advanceCurveShorteningPairs uses a 0.03 rad tolerance (around :3661). Per-tick step size is s = 0.4 m/s divided by fps. At 200+ fps, s is about 6.7 mm.

Concluded (derived in issue 66): at distance h from the chord, the corner deficit is roughly h(1/a + 1/b), where a and b are arc lengths. The fuse is guaranteed to fire only if (s/2)(1/a + 1/b) < 0.03. At high frame rates the free end can oscillate across the chord with amplitude up to s and never satisfy this, which produces the stall. Issue 67 removes this root cause. Issue 66 proposes a smaller fix.

Not tested: the stall was not reproduced in a run.
