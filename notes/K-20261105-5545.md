## Observed
At step 35, inspection identified that tie/detach rejects two incident selections with the same `geodesicId`, while straightening only supports two segments in one cell. The agent connected these limits to portal-wrapped loops and selections of two ends of the same geodesic.

## Conclusion
A local relaxation of the same-`geodesicId` check is insufficient. The model needs to represent the specific incident end, including its segment and portal lift, so tie and shortening can distinguish two ends belonging to one geodesic.
