## Observed
- A throwaway script (step 38) moved a body toward a cell front with `ignoreForbiddenZones` set to true and to false.
- With zones ignored: the body reached about (9.65, -9.65) with 0 crossings and 0 blocked moves, and the minimum signed distance to sides was -2.152 (outside the cell).
- With zones enforced: the body stopped near (7.25, -7.25) with 68 blocked moves.
- The same agent later saved related findings as issue files 61-65 using Write (steps 47, 48, 57, 58, 63), not know_write.

## Concluded
- In this case the forbidden-zone check, not the side-wall check, is what keeps the body inside the cell. Ignoring zones lets the body leave the cell through the corner region.
- Any change to forbidden-zone behavior should be tested with this scenario, since the result depends on the flag.
