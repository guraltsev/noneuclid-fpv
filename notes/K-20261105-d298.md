- Maintainer, 2026-11-05: a free geodesic is not destroyed at a corner; it remembers its length and extends immediately after the corner is cleared. A corner glow comes later.
- Do not confuse this with the flow: a tied curve the flow cannot shorten is destroyed, for any block reason, and `unresolve` is gone.
- Within one rotate or aim session the rule already holds through `geodesicCannonRotationTargetLengthMeters`, pinned by the test "rebuilds a rotated geodesic to its remembered length after clearing a forbidden zone". The value is client state and ends with the tool: issue 93, job 64 moves it to the arc.
- `breakOnForbiddenZone` (the carry rebuild's destroy) was removed.
