On 2026-11-01 the maintainer reversed Decision 1 of job 26 ("resolve in one tick, no animation"): tying geodesics must start a curve shortening **flow** over many frames, and the state is real (the registry is the current piecewise-geodesic curve at every tick), not a presentation over a precomputed answer. `advanceCurveShorteningPairs` moves every free vertex along the descent direction (sum of the two unit tangents, lifted into the vertex's cell frame) by `min(speed*dt, exact line-search minimum)`; the line search is what the job-06 descent lacked (it stalled with a fixed step). Rules from the maintainer: the vertex count never grows (no vertex insertion), the length never grows, a taut vertex (turning angle < 5e-3 rad) is removed and its arcs merge, a numerical limit is fine.

Consequences: `resolveCurveShorteningPair`, `sleeve.ts` (sleeve/funnel/taut string) and `GeodesicCornerObject` were deleted (nothing creates corners without vertex insertion). A curve that would need a wrap vertex at a forbidden-zone disk ends `unresolved`; building that (corner insertion) is the open follow-up. `CurveShorteningPairObject` holds only `vertexIds`; the arcs are the moving intervals incident to those vertices.

Testing trap: a debug Edge window that is `hidden` throttles timers and stops animation frames between CDP commands; `Emulation.setFocusEmulationEnabled` lasts only while the session is attached, so sample the flow inside ONE attached session (a driver that evaluates, waits, then reads state), not with separate `cdp eval` calls.
