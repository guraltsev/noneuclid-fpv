Torus-skew cell map A sends u to u' and v to v' (u=(w,0), v=(a,b)). Walls, bodies and headings all use the full A, so bodies keep their angle to the room edges, and A=RS has a rotation part R that only comes from pinning u to the x-axis. Example: a 0 to w at b=w turns v by 45 degrees though a=w is the same flat torus (Dehn twist). Stripping R from headings alone would break alignment with the room. The floor is painted by rigid patches anchored on the tile lattice (floorPatchSamples in src/cell-complex/floorUv.ts is the spec of the shader in src/render/three/floorPatchMaterial.ts): UV G(p-A) with G fixed from the base state, so pebbles keep their shape, seamless across u and v, equal to the rest floor after a Dehn twist. Step size is a fixed Teichmuller length 1/375. Documented in _docs/MATHEMATICAL_MODEL.md (Headings, Floor texture). Evidence: tests 1-24 of job 67 pass; the browser sweep to a=8 and a=15 was NOT done (headless tab never stepped the deformation, geometry.state current stayed 0), so the look is unchecked. Also: three.js Material.clone() does not copy onBeforeCompile or customProgramCacheKey, so the floor patch must run on each clone; the clip patch now keeps an earlier program key.
