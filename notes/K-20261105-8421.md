## Observed
- Batch runs such as `npm run smoke -- --world cube,icosahedron,torus-moduli,genus-2-torus` failed for different subsets of worlds on different runs (icosahedron and torus-moduli, then cube and genus-2-torus). Each failure was 'loading screen still present after 60000 ms; no canvas'.
- The same worlds were rerun one at a time with `npm run smoke -- -world <name>` in a loop, and every world passed (`SMOKE OK`).

## Concluded
- The failure is likely environmental (load time or resource contention when several worlds share one run), not caused by the change. The agent did not confirm the cause.
