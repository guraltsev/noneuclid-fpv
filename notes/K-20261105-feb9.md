Found while checking whether the curve shortening rate depends on player movement: advanceCurveShorteningPairs reads no player state; its step is speed*min(dt,0.1) with dt = clock.getDelta() capped at 0.1 s (createThreeApp.ts ~1076), so only frame rate below ~10 fps (plausibly lower while moving) slows it. 012 P8 originally said the host chooses the step size and rejected a fixed tick; on 2026-11-05 the maintainer revised it (see 012 P8 and change log): simulation time is a simulation setting, shell shows state at any frame rate, load policy raises h up to a max instead of lagging/queuing, no time-discarding cap. 013 (step, flow speedLaw cap at line ~1094) and the queued engine jobs 48-55 are not yet aligned. Not yet measured in the running app.
