Observed: at the portal-hit path in geodesicCannon.ts, the target start is set to T(hit) + eps*d' with portalStartEpsilonMeters = 1e-4 (around :1373). The remaining-length budget (around :1505) subtracts only trace.lengthMeters, and the next trace restarts from the nudged point (around :1513). Nothing subtracts the nudge itself.

Concluded: summed over k portal crossings, the realized path is longer than the requested length by roughly k * 1e-4 m. This is the drift described in issue 69. The fix direction is to account for the nudge in the budget or to restart from the unnudged point.
