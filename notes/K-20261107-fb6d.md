## Observed
- A probe batch (job 62 review) ran the direct-write detector against small probe files in render/. The probe `zzP00control.ts` (plain write) was caught, and `zzP20alias.ts` (aliased write) was caught.
- `zzP01destructure.ts` (destructuring write) and `zzP02element.ts` (element-access write) were reported as MISSED, with w=0 and m=0.
- The detector's output reported 24 direct-write calls and 16 mutator uses across 8 files for the batch. The step-2 output was consistent with this, but the probes show that the count can undercount.

## Concluded
- A clean result from the direct-write detector does not prove that no client module writes world state. Destructuring and element-access writes can pass unseen.
- Any review that says 'no unlisted client writes' should either extend the detector to these forms or say that it covers only plain and aliased writes.
