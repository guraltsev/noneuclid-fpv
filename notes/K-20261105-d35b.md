Observed: after the carry module was made to actually run in the oracle test (the test's fake now steps the world through runFrame), the property test `match fresh refreshes for locked geodesics through carries of any cannon` failed with `write guard: module carry wrote "geodesicMeasurements/length", which it does not declare`. The stack goes from carry/rebuildEmitterGeodesics through rebuildConnectedGeodesicBetweenEmitters and rebuildLockedGeodesicFromEndpointsAndPortalWordDetailed to removeGeodesicIntervalAndDerivedObjects in world-objects/geodesic/segments.ts, which calls registry.remove on the length objects.

Concluded: the carry module's declared writes (writesCoreKinds in carry/module.ts) cover the geodesic model kinds but not the measurement kind that the rebuild removes. The oracle test had passed before only because the carry never ran in it. The fix was left open and recorded in issue 94 (_issues/94_removing_a_geodesic_writes_measurements_from_any_module.md), not decided here.
