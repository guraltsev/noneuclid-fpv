Observed: with a median over 9 samples, an alternating two-period cadence produced changing h in the simulation clock tests. After the repair, the median uses 10 samples and takes the mean of the two middle values; the test 'does not read the first frames as a load they have not sustained' then passed, and the 60/90 Hz sweeps kept h at one distinct value in steady conditions.

Concluded: the even window with a mean of the middle pair keeps h stable on alternating cadences. An odd window does not. The sweep was run in the scratch test and is not a committed guard.
