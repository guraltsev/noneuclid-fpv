World map (design 015). Each face lifts what stands on it along its own normal, and two lifted lines meet over an edge only if the geodesic crosses it square on, so at any height a ray is straight OR connected. Tried and failed: rays at beam height (gaps at edges); rays flat with tall emitters (ray detached from emitter); mitering ends to P + h(nA+nB)/(1+nA.nB) (connected, but visibly kinked on every face at h = 1.08 m). Resolved by giving nothing on the map any height: sprites rendered once from the real meshes (runtimeObjectRootsById, geodesic sources, avatar model) into a WebGLRenderTarget, plus flat strips in the ribbon material. A render-target sprite holds premultiplied colour: use premultipliedAlpha, because an alphaTest cut-out loses thin parts as mipmaps thin them. Anchor a sprite by the projection of the model's base, not its picture centre, or an emitter sits off its rays. Evidence: headless screenshots of cube, octahedron and dodecahedron, 2026-11-06. Not verified on a headset. Separately: a temporary probe patch in src/ reaches the user's running vite dev server, so build probes from a copy into dist.
