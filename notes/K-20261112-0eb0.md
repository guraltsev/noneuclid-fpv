Evidence, 2026-11-12 on b054bab:
- createThreeApp.ts frame loop: `if (frame.resetRequested) { scriptedMotion.cancel(...); teleportPlayerHome(); resetRuntimeWorld(); }`. desktopControls sets resetRequested on KeyR; the XR hint table binds reset to 'Stick press'. The tutorial labels it 'start over: removes everything you placed'. By contrast, the menu Reload needs two presses.
- Observed in the running app: after one left click with the Geodesic emitter tool, dumpAllGeodesics().selectedTool was 'none' and the HUD badge vanished, with no message.
- Also observed: right click opens the tool menu only when the aim hits nothing focusable; no key opens the tool menu regardless of aim (Esc only closes).
See _docs/design/017-discoverable-ui-and-level-design.md, findings F6, F8 and F10.
