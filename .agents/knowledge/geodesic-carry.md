# Carrying an emitter of a locked geodesic

Invariant: a geodesic interval's portal word must chain from its start anchor's cell to its end anchor's cell. Anything that changes an anchor's cell without extending the word breaks the geodesic silently (rebuilds fail, the carry mode keeps the stale geodesic, release deletes it with no console message). Symptom seen: after picking up an emitter that lives on another face than the player, then releasing, the geodesic is gone.

- The only way to change the cell of an emitter that anchors a locked geodesic is `moveEmitterAlongRoute` (`world-objects/geodesic/cannon.ts`): all or nothing, it refuses (and reports) a move the route does not explain.
- Who is carried is `session.carry` (`{ objectId }`, set by `begin-carry` after `enter-tool-mode`, dropped by the reducer when the carry tool is left); the tool reads the id from there, not from `cannonToolState.selectedCannonId`. One carrier per object is not enforced anywhere (the contract is stateless; interleaved carriers are safe but erratic). `geodesicCannonCarry.test.ts` dispatches commands and drops their effects, so the tool's release (`finishGeodesicCannonCarry`) cannot rely on a host effect.
- The carry rules live in `world-objects/carry.ts` (job 47), not in the tool: the carrier sends displacement steps (at most 1 m, never merged), the emitter's reaction is `reactToDisplacement`, and the portals crossed are read off the segment walk of each step. The tool only locates the hand (`locateCarrierTarget`; a wall stops the hand just short), runs `carryTowardTarget` and stores each accepted move through `moveEmitterAlongRoute`. The emitter, not the tool, decides what a zone does, and a junction's exclusion zone is a wall (maintainer, 2026-11-01): a step that would enter the zone widened by the body radius, or sweep through it, is cut short just before it (`carryZoneMarginMeters`, the movement layer's skin, 1e-6 m) and the object stays carried; nothing is destroyed or detached because of a zone, and a hand held beyond the zone keeps the emitter at its edge until the hand moves away or lets go. The geometry is the shared movement code (`collectDevelopedObstacles` + `sweepDiscAgainstObstacle`, as for creatures); a creature slides along the zone, a carried object stops. Walls differ on purpose: the carried anchor stops `carryWallMarginMeters` (0.02 m) before a wall, the body radius is not used for walls. A zone uses the body radius (`CarriedPlace.radiusMeters`, which the tool takes from the emitter's collision bounds), not the collision shape's height. What happens to the emitter's geodesics afterwards is also the emitter's rule, in `world-objects/geodesic/carryEnd.ts`: `rebuildEmitterGeodesics` (each carried frame, with the carry's safety net), and `releaseCarriedEmitter` (final rebuild + prune, for a put-down). Each returns the ids it touched; the tool only refreshes overlays, the glow, the tool selection and the palette. A kick-away for touching a zone, planned for later, would be a new outcome of `reactToDisplacement` and a change in that one function.
- `findNearestImageRoute` (used by `displacementToTarget` to pick the glide direction) must validate that the straight segment to the chosen image crosses exactly the route's portals. Without it, a route that winds around a cube corner can have a nearer image and gets picked. It also means a hand "straight below" an emitter near a cube corner can be reached round the other side of the corner: the nearest development wins.
- A place on the surface does not determine the way round, so the glide toward the hand needs a convention: the nearest valid straight development (searched up to 6 portals; the short search of 2 is used when it finds the target within 1 m). A pickup is not a jump: the emitter glides at no more than 20 m/s when the tool gets `deltaSeconds`; callers without time (the carry tests) get unlimited travel per call, still in steps of at most 1 m.
- Test trap: in `tests/render/geodesicCannonCarry.test.ts` the player must stand somewhere a player could, with the hand elsewhere. A player placed exactly at the hand finds no crossing at all and makes every release look broken (I once "reproduced" the bug that way, and it was a test artifact).
- Reproducing a live bug: record to disk as it happens. A recorder that lives only in the page is lost when the window closes.
- The highlight of a carried object is a translucent shape drawn **on the object itself**, not at the hand (`render/three/carryHighlight.ts`; maintainer, 2026-11-01): a stuck emitter (stopped short of a wall or zone) must look stuck, with the gap to the hand visible. **What the shape is, is the object's own business** (`carryHighlight` on the object, type in `world-objects/carryHighlightShape.ts`): a rough approximation of its own shape, an ellipsoid for the emitter (`cannonCarryHighlight` in `geodesic/objects.ts`, sized from the drawn model and not from the collision cylinder: `Post.glb` and `Lightsaber.glb` at the renderer's scale and placement are 1.166 m tall from the floor and reach 0.138 m sideways, the ellipsoid is those half-extents times 1.15; `tests/render/cannonModelBounds.test.ts` re-measures the assets, so a changed model fails it. The post is NOT 1.7 m tall: that was a guess from a description, and the first ellipsoid built on it filled the screen); an object that declares none gets a sphere from its collision radius plus 0.1 m, centred on the collision shape. The renderer knows no object kinds. It is not a scene-root mesh: the instance sync (`runtimeObjectInstanceSync.ts`) adds one record per place where the carried object (`session.carry.objectId`) is drawn, from the object's own render records, so it follows it on every portal path, ghost copy and other face and turns with its yaw. The old glow was a cylinder at the hand in the player's frame; do not bring back anything that guesses the emitter's place from the hand.
- A carried emitter stored on a face whose frame is turned relative to the previous one (cube faces are quarter-turned) must have its pose yaw, `aimYawRadians` and per-geodesic launch yaws turned together: the documented invariant is that `aimYawRadians` equals the yaw of `localPose.rotation`. `emitterAtPose` in `geodesic/cannon.ts` keeps them in step; the tool turns the pose by the rotation of the route it crossed. Picking an emitter up deletes its free (unlocked) geodesics by design, so only locked ones matter when testing this.
- Things drawn in the scene root (emitter labels, length labels) are in the player's frame. The emitter labels and the measured-length label now find where the emitter or the geodesic appears from the player through up to two portals (`findNearestImageRoute`, `enumerateCellImages`); without that they vanish or jump when the player or the object is on another face.
- Letting go of a distant emitter is the **hand's** policy, not the emitter's and not the contract's (`src/interaction/handCarry.ts`; maintainer, 2026-11-01, so that distance force fields can replace it later in that one place). `carryTowardTarget` only reports `remaining` (meters along the valid straight development, or `"unreachable"`); the tool asks `decideHandCarry(remaining, phase, glidingLeash)` after each advance. A wall or an exclusion zone never ends a carry (the emitter stops short and stays in the hand); only distance does. **The leash has two phases** (maintainer, hand test 2026-11-01: one long leash let a stuck emitter sit ~4 m from the hand): while the emitter still glides to the hand after the pickup the leash is loose (`handCarryLeashMeters`: pickup range + hand reach + 0.67 m lag slack, about 3.9 m desktop and 3.2 m headset, so a fresh pickup never drops); the first frame the emitter is within `handCarryArrivalMeters` (0.1 m) of the hand the tool dispatches `carry-arrived` (`session.carry.arrived`), and from then on the leash is `heldCarryLeashMeters` (1.0 m, tunable). A carry that never arrives still ends at the loose leash. Symptom if the arrival flag is lost: a held emitter that is stuck behind a zone is only let go at the loose distance.
